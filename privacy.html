Privacy policy

Effective October 31, 2026

This policy explains what information Armila's Restaurant & Lounge collects through armilaslounge.com, how we use it, and the choices you have. Questions go to info@armilaslounge.com or (289) 674-9692.

Information you send us

When you use the catering inquiry form, we collect your name, phone number, email address and the event details you enter. The form is delivered to our inbox by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), a form delivery service. We use these details to reply to you and plan your event. We do not add you to a mailing list unless you ask us to.

If you email or call us, we receive the information you choose to share and use it to respond.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how people use this site. It records pages viewed, approximate location at the city level, device and browser type, how you arrived, and clicks on our call, order and directions links. It uses cookies to do this. We do not send your name, email or phone number to Google Analytics.

If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, analytics does not load. You can also opt out on any site with Google's opt-out browser add-on.

Maps and other services

The hours and location section embeds a Google Map, which loads when you scroll to it. Google may set cookies and collect information under the Google Privacy Policy.

Links to Uber Eats, Instagram and TikTok take you to sites with their own privacy policies. Orders placed on Uber Eats are handled by Uber, not by this site. This site does not take payments.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent your personal information.
  • We do not use your inquiry details for advertising.
  • We do not collect payment card details on this site.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiry emails for as long as we need them to respond, plan your event and keep normal business records, and then delete them.

Your rights

Under Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA), you can ask what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or withdraw your consent to its use. Email info@armilaslounge.com and we will respond.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update it on this page and change the effective date at the top.
