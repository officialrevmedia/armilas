Questions

Is Armila's halal?

Yes. Every dish is halal, and all meat comes from halal suppliers.

How do I reserve a table?

Call (289) 674-9692. We take reservations by phone, including large groups and game nights.

Do you show live sports?

Yes. NBA, NFL, NHL, MLB, UFC and major soccer matches play on a large screen with full surround sound. Call ahead to book a table for a big game.

How late are you open?

Until 1 AM Sunday to Wednesday, and until 2 AM Thursday to Saturday. We open at 12 PM every day. The kitchen closes one hour before the restaurant.

Is there a minimum spend?

Yes. Dine-in has a $20 minimum spend per person, and tables are held for up to 2.5 hours.

Do you have vegetarian options?

Yes. Veggie bolani, burani banjaan and fatoush salad are vegetarian, and Afghan macaroni can be made without meat.

Can I book Armila's for a private event?

Yes. We host private events for up to 75 guests, with partial or full buyouts. Weddings, nikkahs, birthdays, watch parties and corporate dinners all work. Call (289) 674-9692 to plan it.

Do you offer catering?

Yes. We cater across Hamilton and the GTA for 30 or more guests, with packages from $19 per person. At least 24 hours' notice is preferred.

Do you deliver?

Yes, through Uber Eats. You can also call (289) 674-9692 and order for pickup.

Where are you, and is there parking?

We are at 2200 Rymal Road East, Unit 7, in Hamilton, near Upper Centennial Parkway (Highway 20). Parking on site is free.
