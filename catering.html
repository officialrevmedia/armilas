The dishes

  • Qabli with Meat

    Slow-cooked lamb cubes over Afghan Qabli rice. Made without meat for vegetarian guests.

  • Chalow

    Steamed long-grain basmati rice, lightly seasoned.

  • Shola with Meatballs

    Afghan rice cooked to a creamy, risotto-like texture, with spiced meatballs.

  • Mantu

    Beef dumplings with garlic yogurt and tomato sauce.

  • Chicken Kabab

    Chicken breast in Afghan spices, grilled over open flame.

  • Beef Shami Kabab

    Beef in a house spice blend, grilled over open flame.

  • Chicken Karahi

    Pakistani-style chicken with tomatoes, yogurt and fresh spices.

  • Burani Banjaan

    Fried eggplant on tomato sauce with garlic yogurt.

  • Firni

    Rice pudding with cardamom and saffron, topped with pistachios and almonds.

Catering questions

What is the minimum order?

30 guests for any catering package.

How much notice do you need?

At least 24 hours is preferred. Call (289) 674-9692 to confirm your date.

Is the catering halal?

Yes. Everything we cook is halal.

Can you feed vegetarian guests?

Yes. The Qabli can be made without meat, and burani banjaan is vegetarian and comes in every package. Tell us how many vegetarian guests you expect when you book.

Where do you cater?

Across Hamilton and the GTA.

Can we hold the event at the restaurant instead?

Yes. Armila's hosts private events for up to 75 guests, with partial or full buyouts.
